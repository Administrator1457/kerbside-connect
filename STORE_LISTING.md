# Kerbside Connect — Store Listing Draft

## App name
Kerbside Connect

## Short description
Find and share useful items spotted during kerbside clean-ups in your community.

## Full description
Kerbside Connect helps communities share useful items spotted during kerbside clean-ups so more things can be reused instead of going to waste.

### Find items nearby
Browse recent community reports on a map or search by suburb, street area and item category.

### Share what you find
Create a report with categories, a description, an optional photo and an optional approximate location.

### Built for community reuse
Reports are community-submitted and designed to expire automatically, helping keep the information useful and current.

### Stay safer
Community guidelines, report flagging and moderation tools help people report inappropriate or unsafe content.

### Privacy by design
Location is optional and approximate. Accounts are optional for ordinary use. Account holders can request permanent account deletion from inside the app.

Kerbside Connect is an independent community project. It is not an official council service and does not guarantee that an item will still be available.
