Terms of use

Community information only

Kerbside Connect provides community-submitted information about items seen during kerbside clean-ups. Reports are not guarantees that an item will still be available and are not official council notices.

Use safely and lawfully

Do not enter private property without permission, obstruct roads or footpaths, or collect items that appear unsafe, hazardous or restricted. Follow all applicable laws and council requirements.

Report responsibly

Only submit information you believe is genuine and avoid personal information in descriptions and photos. Kerbside Connect may need to remove reports that are inaccurate, unsafe, abusive or inappropriate.

No guarantee

The app is provided as a community service. Availability, map accuracy, council rules and reported items can change without notice. Check relevant official sources when safety or legal requirements matter.

Privacy & safety

🔒 Your privacy matters

Location is optional. When you choose to attach your location to a report, Kerbside Connect rounds the coordinates before sending them to the database. Do not include house numbers, faces, vehicle registration details or other private information in photos or descriptions.

👤 Accounts

An account is optional. Your email is used by Supabase authentication. Never share your password.

📸 Photos

Only upload photos you are comfortable sharing with the Kerbside Connect community. Photos are currently stored with reports and may be visible to other app users.

Community guidelines

♻️ Keep reports useful

Only report genuine kerbside clean-up items. Respect private property, pedestrians, traffic and council restrictions. Do not post dangerous, illegal, abusive or personally identifying content.

🚩 See something wrong?

Use “Flag report” on the report card and explain the problem. Reports are community submissions and are not council confirmations.

Council information


Kerbside Connect is a community project. Council information should always be checked against the relevant council's official website.
