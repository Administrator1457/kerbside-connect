Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

A few quick answers before you explore Kerbside Connect.

What is Kerbside Connect?

Kerbside Connect is a community app for discovering and sharing reports about useful items left out for kerbside collection.

Does it cost anything to use?

There is currently no charge to access the website or open the app. Standard mobile data charges from your provider may apply.

How do I find items near me?

Open the app and browse the map or available reports. If you choose to enable location access, the app can help sort reports by distance. Location access is optional.

Can I report a useful item?

Yes. Use the app's reporting feature to share a description and, where useful, a photo. Only share information you are comfortable making visible to other users.

Are reports guaranteed to be accurate or still available?

No. Reports are community-submitted and are not council-confirmed. Items may be collected or moved at any time, so check details and use your judgement before travelling.

How should I stay safe and respect privacy?

Never enter private property without permission, obey local rules, and do not include personal details, faces, number plates or other sensitive information in photos or descriptions. Report anything concerning through the app where available.

What happens to my location?

Location access is optional. The app is designed to use approximate report locations rather than publishing your precise device location. Read our privacy policy for more information.

How can I get help or share feedback?

Email us at paynebrian527@gmail.com with “Kerbside Connect” in the subject line.

Frequently asked questions.

A few helpful things to know before you jump in.

What is Kerbside Connect?

Kerbside Connect is a community app for discovering and sharing reports about useful items left out during kerbside clean-ups. It helps neighbours find information about items that may be available nearby.

Is Kerbside Connect free to use?

The app is currently offered as a community project. Any future costs or changes to access will be communicated clearly. Internet or mobile data charges from your provider may apply.

How do I find items near me?

Open the app, browse the map and review the available community reports. Location access is optional; you can explore without enabling it, though nearby sorting may be limited.

How do I share a find?

Use the reporting option in the app to add a useful description and, if appropriate, a photo. Please only report items and locations you can safely and lawfully access.

Are reports verified or guaranteed to be available?

No. Reports are community-submitted and are not necessarily verified by a council or Kerbside Connect. Items may be moved or collected before you arrive, so use your judgement and check the details.

How does Kerbside Connect handle location and privacy?

Location use is optional and the app is designed to use approximate report locations. Please do not post personal details, private information or photos that identify people. Read our privacy policy for more information.

Can I collect anything I see on the map?

Please follow local laws and council rules, respect private property and posted signs, and never enter a property or take items unless you have permission. Safety comes first.

How can I report a problem or contact the team?

Use the report-flagging option in the app where available, or email the Kerbside Connect team with feedback.

Community reports are shared to help neighbours — availability and item condition can change.
