Kerbside Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Kerbside Connect is a community project that helps people discover and share useful items seen during kerbside clean-ups. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, why it is used, how it is protected and how you can request deletion.

1. Information we handle

Account information: If you create an account, Kerbside Connect uses your email address and authentication information through Supabase Authentication. An account is optional for ordinary browsing and reporting.

Community reports: Reports can include suburb, optional street/area information, selected item categories, a description, an optional photo and an optional approximate map location.

Location: Location access is optional. When you attach your device location to a report, the app rounds the coordinates before sending them to the service. Do not include house numbers, faces, registration plates or other private information in reports or photos.

Saved reports: Saved reports are kept locally on your device and are not shared with your account.

2. How information is used

Information is used to provide Kerbside Connect features such as displaying community reports on a map, searching reports, sharing reports, moderating inappropriate content, preventing abuse and maintaining account functionality.

Kerbside Connect does not sell personal information.

3. Service providers

Kerbside Connect uses Supabase for authentication, database, server functions and image storage. The public web app is delivered through GitHub Pages. Map functionality uses Leaflet and OpenStreetMap data/services.

4. Public community information

Community reports are intended to be visible to other users. Do not submit personal, confidential or sensitive information. A report may remain visible until it expires or is removed by moderation.

5. Security

Kerbside Connect uses HTTPS for network traffic and server-side access controls for protected backend operations. Access to moderation functions is restricted by backend permissions. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so please only submit information you are comfortable sharing.

6. Retention and expiry

Community reports are designed to expire after three days unless the service is changed to refresh them. Moderation and security records may be retained as reasonably necessary to operate the service, investigate abuse and protect the community.

7. Account deletion

If you have an account, you can permanently delete it from My Account → Delete my account inside Kerbside Connect. Account deletion removes the authentication account. Community reports and flags are designed to remain usable without exposing the deleted account as their owner.

If you cannot access the app, use the privacy contact mechanism shown on the official Kerbside Connect store listing or website.

8. Children's privacy

Kerbside Connect is a general community service and is not specifically directed to children. Users should not submit another person's personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available at this URL.

10. Privacy contact

For privacy questions or concerns, use the developer contact mechanism provided with the official Kerbside Connect website or app store listing. Please do not post passwords, authentication codes or other sensitive information in a public support request.
